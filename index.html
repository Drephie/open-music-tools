Open Music Tools

Free music tools to learn, practise and make your own music.

Everything runs on your device: nothing is uploaded, no ads, no trackers.

Tools

  • Choordination Play chords with your hands, using your camera. Nothing leaves your device. Beta
  • Piano Play, and see the name of the chord
  • Ear Trainer Lessons and a game to train your ear
  • Metronome A steady click to practise with, and tap tempo
  • Vocal Tuner Sing a note: see if it is too low, too high or in tune
  • Instrument Tuner Tune your guitar, bass, ukulele or violin, string by string
  • Pitch Detector See the note you sing, live, and how your pitch moves
  • Pitch Training Warm-ups: hear them, sing them back, see the notes in tune Beta

Coming soon

  • Chordigits Count chords on your fingers, I to VII, using your camera
  • BPM Finder Find the speed (BPM) of a song you own
  • Chord Finder Find the key and the chords of a song you own
  • Practice Player Slow a song down without changing its pitch, and loop a part
  • Jam Machine Chord progressions in many styles to play and sing over
