# Jam Machine drum hits

Six single hits (kick, snare, cross-stick, closed and open hi-hat, ride) from
**Virtuosity Drums** by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples,
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums, released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Taken from the "mid" microphones (`Samples/mid/...`: kick snon vl3 rr1, snare
center vl24, snare crossstick vl10, hh closed vl3 rr1, hh open vl2 rr1, ride
vl2 rr1), made mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit, trimmed after the hit with a short fade,
and each brought to the same peak level. The Jam Machine sets each drum's level.
